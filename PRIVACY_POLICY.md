## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Quick Heroes 11** ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a safe, transparent, and 100% free grassroots cricket scoring and match management platform.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how you have full control over your personal data.

---

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide cricket match scoring, team roster management, and player profile statistics:

1. **Profile & Registration Data (Optional / User-Provided):**
   - **Name:** Your cricketer or team name (e.g., Kasam Jat, Latif).
   - **Mobile Number:** Provided during registration or player addition to identify scorers and verify profiles via one-time PIN (OTP).
   - **City / Town:** Your cricket playing region (e.g., Naliya, Bhuj, Kutch).
   - **Cricket Role & Jersey Number:** Your playing role (Batsman, Bowler, All-rounder, Wk-Batsman) and preferred jersey number.
   - **Profile Photo / Avatar:** Photos selected using the system Android Photo Picker, or standard preset cricket avatars.

2. **Cricket Performance & Match Data:**
   - Runs scored, balls faced, boundaries (4s & 6s), wickets taken, overs bowled, catches, run-outs, match results, and team scores.
   - Match schedules, overs, toss decisions, and ball-by-ball commentary feeds.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use your information exclusively for the following purposes:
- To calculate and display real-time live match scores, run rates, and partnerships.
- To maintain your cricket career statistics, highest score, best bowling figures, and leaderboard rankings.
- To auto-save added squad players so you never have to re-enter them in subsequent matches.
- To distinguish match scorers from view-only spectators for fair scoring integrity.

---

## 4. Permissions & Device Access
Quick Heroes 11 adheres strictly to the Principle of Least Privilege:
- **No Storage Permission Required:** We use Android's zero-permission Photo Picker (`ActivityResultContracts.PickVisualMedia`) for avatar selection. We do NOT request or access `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` or `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`.
- **No Background Location Tracking:** We do not track or collect your GPS location.
- **No Contacts Access:** We do not read or upload your device contact book.
- **No Camera Hardware Access Without Intent:** Photos are chosen exclusively via system dialogs.

---

## 5. Data Sharing & Third Parties
- **Zero Third-Party Data Selling:** We DO NOT sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information or cricket data to advertisers or third-party data brokers.
- **No Behavioral Tracking / No Third-Party Ad SDKs:** Quick Heroes 11 is 100% free and contains no tracking pixels or invasive advertising SDKs.

---

## 6. User Rights, Data Retention & Account Deletion
You maintain complete control over your data:
- **Account Deletion:** You can permanently delete your account and all associated profile details at any time directly within the app:
  `Drawer Menu -> Account Settings -> Delete account`.
- **Clear Data:** You can reset local match caches, saved rosters, and statistics anytime via:
  `Drawer Menu -> Account Settings -> Clear data`.
- **Edit Profile:** You can update your name, phone number, city, or career numbers anytime from your profile screen.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy
Quick Heroes 11 is designed for cricket enthusiasts of all ages, including young athletes participating in school and gully cricket matches. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 beyond what is entered for team lineups and match scoring.
